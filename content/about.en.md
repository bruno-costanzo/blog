## Hello, I'm Bruno Costanzo

Ruby on Rails developer. Specialized in TDD, state machines, and design patterns.

### My Experience

I'm currently working as a **Full Stack Developer** at **Renuo AG** since July 2025, a software agency specialized in Ruby on Rails based in Zurich, Switzerland.

Previously, I worked remotely for **Unagi Software** (2022-2025), a service company that provided development services to various international companies. During my 3 years there, I participated in several projects as the lead developer, including **Sayge** (professional coaching platform), **Blackbook Travels** (hotel management), **Patrimore**, and various internal projects like **DOFF** (license management) and **Ruberto** (an open source gem that integrates Uber with Rails).

### Technologies

I work primarily with **Ruby, Rails, RSpec, Kamal, PostgreSQL, SQL, Hotwire, and Stimulus**.

### Languages

I am fluent in English and Spanish, with intermediate Italian.

### Location

I currently live in **Zurich, Switzerland**.
